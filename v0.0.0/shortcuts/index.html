Shortcuts

Keyboard

Every exercise can be answered without the mouse. When you choose an exercise from the list, the keyboard goes to it, so its keys work at once. Tab and Shift+Tab move between the buttons, and Space presses the one that has the focus. Hover over any button to see its key.

Choosing an answer

Keys What it does
1 to 9 Press the matching answer button, counting from the left. Available when an exercise has nine answers or fewer.
C D E F G A B Keyboard Identification and Circle of Fifths: the natural note or key of that name. A minor key is typed as the major key of its letter.
Shift+a letter, Ctrl+a letter Keyboard Identification and Circle of Fifths: the sharp (Shift) or the flat (Ctrl) of that note or key.
1 to 6 Circle of Fifths, Roman numerals: I, ii, iii, IV, V and vi. For a key with two names (such as B and C♭) press the key of each name.
1 to 9 Keyboard Identification with the octave asked: the octave, typed as its number.
A S D F Arpeggio identification: the inversion, which is the second row of buttons. The chord type is on 1 to 4.

Placing notes on the staff (Note Construction and Scale Construction)

Clicking the staff is never needed. A pale note, the caret, shows the note that would be placed, and the line under the staff says what comes next, in words that a screen reader can read.

Keys Button What it does
↑ / ↓ Higher / Lower Move the note one line or space up or down. The first press starts from the middle line of the staff.
Page Up / Page Down Octave ↑ / Octave ↓ Move the note an octave up or down.
A to G   Jump to the nearest note with that name, above or below.
← / →   Step backwards or forwards through no sign, ♯, ♭ and ♮ (♮ only when the staff has a key signature).
+ - 0 ♯ ♭ ♮ Choose a sharp, a flat or a natural for the next note.
Return Place note Place the note at the caret.
Backspace Undo Scale Construction: take back the last note or step.

Once all the notes of a scale are placed, and the Steps setting is on, the same keys mark the steps:

Keys Button What it does
W / H W / H Choose a whole or a half step.
↑ ↓ ← →   Switch between whole and half.
Return Mark step Mark the step and move on to the next pair of notes.

When everything is placed, Return presses Answer. Return always does the next thing: it places a note or marks a step while there is something to place, and answers once there is not.

Building a key signature (Key Signature Construction)

Keys What it does
↑ or → One more sharp (or one fewer flat).
↓ or ← One more flat (or one fewer sharp).
Home Back to the natural sign: no sharps or flats.
Return Answer.

Everywhere

Keys What it does
Esc Go back from the statistics or database page.
Ctrl+Alt+S Open the app settings.
Ctrl+R Restart Alto.
Ctrl+Q Quit.

On macOS, Qt shows these shortcuts with ⌘ in place of Ctrl.

Mouse

Action Where What it does
Click the staff Note Construction, Scale Construction Place the note.
Right-click the staff Note Construction and Scale Construction with accidentals on Step through no sign, sharp, flat and natural.
Turn the wheel over the staff Scale Construction Step through the signs while notes are placed, then switch between whole and half steps.
Double-click a row Database: Answers or Questions Preview that question.
Click a column heading Database Sort by that column.
Hover a chart Statistics Show the exact values for that period.
Drag the edge of the sidebar Exercise screens Resize the exercise list, or drag it narrower than its minimum to hide it.

The menu

The ☰ button at the top left holds Statistics, Browse database, Lessons, Profile, Settings…, Export backup…, Import backup…, Check for updates…, Restart and Quit.
