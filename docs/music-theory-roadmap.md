## Alpha phase

Alto is in alpha. **Complete** means the feature is available in the current development version. **Planned** marks the proposed development sequence. **Under consideration** marks a candidate for future work.

Interval construction comes first, followed by chord construction and staff-to-keyboard practice. Ear training and rhythm come next. Both provide the groundwork for melodic dictation and harmonic analysis.

## Beta phase

Alto is developed with the help of artificial intelligence. The main goal for beta is to replace prose generated by large language models with human-written text throughout the app and its documentation. Lessons, exercise prompts, and feedback should sound like a teacher explaining an idea to a student. The editorial review will also check definitions, musical examples, and answers for accuracy and correct errors where needed.

A second goal is to refactor the codebase so it is easier to read, test, and maintain. That work includes simplifying complicated code, removing duplication, and using tests to verify behavior as the implementation changes.
