## Instrument recordings

The piano is the [Salamander Grand Piano](https://sfzinstruments.github.io/pianos/salamander) V3 by Alexander Holm, a Yamaha C5
grand recorded with two AKG C414 microphones, used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) license. Alto uses 30 of its recordings from one
dynamic layer, converted to mono, trimmed, faded and levelled. The author does not endorse Alto.

Lawrence Fritts created the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios’ sample collection. Andrew Thierauf played
marimba and vibraphone, Sonja Feig played flute, Jessica Wiskus played French horn, and Manuel Tabora played viola.
The studio permits use in any project. Alto converted the recordings to mono, trimmed them, and added fades.
[University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples](https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html)

## Score excerpts

Notation Identification uses the piano parts from Schubert’s *Erlkönig* and Schumann’s *Schöne Fremde* and *Die Lotosblume*.
OpenScore Lieder contributors transcribed the historical editions and released their transcriptions under CC0 1.0.
Alto engraved eight short passages from those transcriptions. [OpenScore Lieder catalogue](https://fourscoreandmore.org/openscore/lieder/) · [CC0 dedication](https://github.com/OpenScore/Lieder/blob/main/LICENSE.txt)
