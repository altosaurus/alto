Instrument recordings

The piano is the Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, a Yamaha C5 grand recorded with two AKG C414 microphones, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence. Alto uses 30 of its recordings from one dynamic layer, converted to mono, trimmed, faded and levelled. The author does not endorse Alto.

The flute, French horn, viola, marimba and vibraphone use recordings from the Musical Instrument Samples of the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, which the studio makes freely available for any project. Alto converted them to mono, trimmed and faded them.
