Learn music theory with Alto

Alto provides exercises and lessons on music theory, optimized with spaced repetition.

Download Alto

What you can practise

  • Note IdentificationName notes on the grand staff or on any of seven clefs, treble to baritone.
  • Keyboard IdentificationName the highlighted key on a piano keyboard.
  • Note ConstructionClick the staff where a named note belongs.
  • Interval IdentificationName the interval between two notes.
  • Key Signature IdentificationName the key from its signature on a staff, in major, minor and the modes.
  • Key Signature ConstructionBuild the key signature of a named key with a fork of sharps and flats.
  • Scale SpellingSpell a degree of a scale or mode.
  • Scale ConstructionPlace the notes of a named scale on a blank staff, one after another.
  • Circle of FifthsFind relative majors and minors, and the Roman numerals of a key, on the circle of fifths.
  • Chord QualityTell triads and seventh chords apart.
  • Arpeggio IdentificationName the type and the inversion of a broken chord.

What makes it different

  • It learns from your answers. Every answer is recorded under your profile. Questions you miss, or take a long time over, come back more often. Questions you answer quickly and correctly fade into the background. How it works.
  • Statistics and a database browser. Charts of accuracy, speed and volume, plus a calendar heatmap of a year of practice, filterable by exercise and timeframe, plus a table view of everything stored. Statistics.
  • Notation and keyboards your way. Eleven notation styles, including Finale Maestro, Leland, Bravura and two handwritten jazz styles, and eleven keyboard looks from a modern piano to a French harpsichord. Settings.
  • Local and private. Your history is one file on your computer. Your data.
  • Free and open source under the MIT License.
